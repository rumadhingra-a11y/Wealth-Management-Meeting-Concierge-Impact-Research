RECOMMENDATIONS

Top 7 UX recommendations

Ranked by adoption impact. Each carries a build horizon so design, product, and engineering know when it lands.

1

Split the dashboard into two tabs

Separate the strategic "Book of Business" view (AUM, net revenue, segment health) from the tactical "AI Adoption & Performance" view (Meeting Concierge, prompt usage). One screen answers "are we growing," the other answers "is the tool working" — bundling them buries both signals.

Immediate Haley · Mercer
2

Add a manual complexity-weighting toggle

AUM alone is a broken proxy for advisor workload — a newly onboarded "baby" client can demand far more hands-on time than a large, dormant account. Let advisors manually weight client intensity so capacity reporting reflects reality.

Immediate Haley · Mercer
3

Deploy peer stack ranking visuals

Show advisors where they sit relative to peers on adoption and production. Ranking is a stronger adoption lever than any tutorial — it instantly flags who needs coaching and who can informally mentor others.

Immediate Timotei · Comerica
4

Add hover-over transparency on every metric

Attach a hover-over definition to AUM, Productivity, and every derived metric explaining exactly how it's calculated. Trust in the number is a prerequisite for trust in the tool — this is the cheapest fix with the highest credibility payoff.

Immediate Haley · Mercer
5

Build a real-time incentive-to-paycheck feed

Make compensation the dashboard's spine: show, in plain terms, how today's activity — completing a portfolio review, closing a wallet-share gap — flows through to the advisor's next paycheck. This is the single strongest adoption hook across every interview.

Short-term Timotei · Comerica
6

Set automated segment alert thresholds

Scope attrition-risk alerts specifically to UHNW and HNW tiers rather than a flat rule for all clients. A missed meeting means a routine service gap at mass-affluent tier, but a systemic risk at UHNW — the alerting logic should escalate accordingly.

Short-term Haley · Mercer
7

Ship AI as headless, embeddable components

Meet advisors inside the tools they already live in — Outlook, custom CRM tabs — instead of forcing a move into a standardized shell. This avoids the FSC migration "no-go" that stalls adoption at firms with heavy custom builds. Cognicore is the benchmark to beat.

Long-term Brian Schmail · The Mather Group
